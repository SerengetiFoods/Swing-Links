Ball Machine

Match: play a computer opponent with real tennis scoring, and you don't need to look. A voice calls every ball, it sounds on the side it's coming to (wear headphones), and beep, beep, BEEP tells you when to hit. Wide balls make you take a step or two to reach them. Swing the right stroke, forehand or backhand.

Practice: a ball machine feeds you for points: 1 in, 2 deep, 3 corner.

Phone motion: hold your phone like a racket handle and swing through. In a match, the game first asks for one forehand and one backhand so it can tell your strokes apart. Swipe: flick right to left for a forehand, left to right for a backhand (mirrored if you play left-handed). Give yourself room to move and swing.
